# Atmosynq World Asset Sources

Atmosynq bundles selected third-party photographic scene bases under the Pexels license. The app modifies/resizes these scene bases and layers live weather effects, interaction regions, parallax, color treatment, and UI over them.

Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

## Product rules
- Do not treat stock scene bases as location photography.
- Do not imply the scene depicts the user's exact address.
- Scene bases are atmosphere archetypes selected from broad weather/location context.
- Keep photographer/source metadata with the repo when assets are replaced or expanded.
- Prefer original Atmosynq-owned/generated art for final production packs when available.
